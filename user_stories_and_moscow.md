# Agile Backlog and MoSCoW Prioritization

The Excel workbook contains the detailed backlog and acceptance criteria. Priorities below are proposed for a hypothetical first release and should be confirmed with the product owner and stakeholders.

- **Must Have:** eligibility decision, policy exceptions, return status, refund calculation, secure payout choice, risk routing, pickup QC, audit trail.
- **Should Have:** exchange incentive, refund tracking, customer dispute escalation.
- **Could Have:** returnless refund for qualifying low-value damaged goods.
- **Won't Have in Phase 1:** cash payout from offline franchise POS drawers.

Story points are relative planning estimates, not hours or delivery commitments. The estimates in the workbook are provisional and should be reconciled with the agreed release scope before sprint planning. The Won't Have story is excluded from the first-release estimate.

These are proposed user stories. They have not been approved by a real product owner or implemented in a live system.
